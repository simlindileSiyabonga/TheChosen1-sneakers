About The Chosen 1 Sneakers

Discover our story, passion, and commitment to redefining sneaker culture with style and quality.

Our Story

Founded in Vanderbijlpark, South Africa, The Chosen 1 Sneakers was born from a love for sneaker culture and a desire to create footwear that stands out. Our journey began at Vaal University of Technology, where a group of passionate designers and sneaker enthusiasts came together to craft unique, high-quality sneakers that blend street style with comfort.

Every pair we create tells a story of individuality, creativity, and bold expression. We’re not just a brand—we’re a movement for those who dare to be different.

Our Mission

At The Chosen 1 Sneakers, our mission is to empower sneaker lovers to express their unique style through innovative designs and premium craftsmanship. We strive to deliver footwear that combines fashion, comfort, and durability, while fostering a community of creatives and trendsetters.

Our Values

  • Creativity: We push boundaries with bold designs that inspire self-expression.
  • Quality: Every sneaker is crafted with premium materials for lasting comfort.
  • Community: We celebrate the sneaker culture that brings us together.
  • Sustainability: We’re committed to eco-friendly practices in our production process.
